I

Executive Summary

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is a premier, founder-led collection of three boutique waterfront hotels operating across the Gulf Coast. Since its founding in 2011, the company has evolved from a single property into a respected independent operator commanding the #2 market position by RevPAR in its region. The business delivers consistent, high-quality hospitality through award-winning culinary offerings and exceptional service, supported by a loyal customer base demonstrating a 41% repeat guest rate.

Key Investment Highlights

  • Strong Market Position: #2 independent boutique operator by RevPAR with 92% average occupancy and a 4.7/5 online reputation across 6,200+ reviews.
  • Exceptional Operational Performance: 41% repeat guest rate indicates loyal clientele and strong brand affinity; 24% employee turnover significantly below the hospitality industry average of 35%.
  • Diversified Revenue: Multi-channel booking strategy (38% direct website, 35% OTAs, 27% repeat/referral) mitigates dependency on any single channel; F&B program contributes 34% of total revenue.
  • Scalable Infrastructure: Standardized SOPs and centralized revenue management enable rapid onboarding of new properties within 90 days; cloud-based PMS across all locations.
  • Growth Pipeline: Management has identified two acquisition targets for 4th and 5th properties; opportunity to expand corporate/bleisure segment from 10% to 18–20% of bookings.
  • Clean Capital Structure: $2,100,000 outstanding mortgage at 5.4% fixed (maturing 2031); 100% privately held with no outside investors.

Financial Summary

Anchor & Vine has demonstrated consistent top-line growth over the past three fiscal years, with revenue increasing from $11,800,000 in FY2023 to $14,200,000 in FY2025—representing a compound annual growth rate of approximately 9.6%. Adjusted EBITDA improved from $3,180,000 in FY2024 to $3,950,000 in FY2025, yielding a robust 27.8% margin and underscoring the business's operational efficiency and pricing power.

II

Company Overview

History & Background

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group was founded in 2011 by Maria Chen and David Okoye, two seasoned hospitality operators with a shared vision of delivering exceptional guest experiences through locally inspired, farm-to-table dining and premium waterfront accommodations. The company began with a single property and has systematically grown to operate three freehold boutique hotels across the Florida Gulf Coast. Over 14 years, the founders have built a brand recognized for its commitment to sustainable sourcing, guest-first service, and deep community partnerships.

Ownership Structure

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is 100% privately held, organized as a single Delaware LLC holding company with three property-level subsidiaries. There are no outside investors. The company has maintained founder control throughout its operational history.

Locations & Facilities

Anchor & Vine operates three freehold waterfront properties across premium Florida Gulf Coast markets:

  • Clearwater Property: Waterfront location with spa services, on-site restaurant, and event venues.

  • Naples Property: Luxury waterfront hotel with on-site restaurant and event facilities.

Combined Capacity: 212 keys across all three properties. All properties have been fully renovated within the past five years, maintaining premium brand standards and guest amenities.

  • Event & Wedding Venues: Dedicated event spaces at all three properties, supporting small to mid-size weddings and corporate functions.

  • Strong Online Reputation: 4.7/5 average rating across 6,200+ guest reviews reflects exceptional service delivery and guest satisfaction.

IV

Operations

Hotel Operations & Guest Services

Each of Anchor & Vine's three properties operates as a full-service boutique hotel delivering premium waterfront experiences. Operations are coordinated at the corporate level while maintaining property-level autonomy for local market responsiveness. Daily operations encompass front office, housekeeping, maintenance, concierge services, and guest relations. Rooms, F&B outlets, spa facilities, and event spaces are actively managed to maximize guest satisfaction and revenue per available room.

Technology & Equipment

  • Cloud-Based Property Management System (PMS): Cloudbeds platform deployed across all three properties, enabling real-time occupancy management, revenue optimization, and guest data integration.
  • Centralized Revenue Management: Unified system for pricing strategy, inventory allocation, and booking channel coordination across all properties.
  • Guest Communication Platform: Integrated system for pre-arrival, in-stay, and post-stay guest communications.
  • Point-of-Sale (POS): F&B and spa revenue capture integrated with corporate financial reporting.

Supply Chain & Vendor Management

F&B operations are supported by a diversified vendor network designed to minimize supply concentration risk and support the company's sustainable sourcing philosophy:

  • Regional Vendor Network: F&B sourced from 6 regional vendors under annual contracts.
  • Vendor Concentration: No single vendor exceeds 18% of COGS, providing resilience and negotiating flexibility.
  • Local Partnerships: Emphasis on seasonal, local ingredients supports community engagement and brand authenticity.

Quality Certifications & Standards

  • AAA Four Diamond Rating: 2 of 3 properties currently maintain AAA Four Diamond certification, reflecting premium service and facility standards.
  • Liquor, Food Service, & Event Licenses: All licenses current and in good standing at all three locations.

Scalability & Growth-Ready Infrastructure

Anchor & Vine has designed operations for rapid scalability, enabling efficient integration of new properties:

  • Standardized SOPs: Comprehensive operating procedures documented and deployed across all properties, ensuring consistency and facilitating knowledge transfer.
  • Training Program: Structured onboarding and continuous training program for new staff and management teams.
  • 90-Day Property Integration: Proven capability to onboard new properties to full operational standards within 90 days of acquisition.

VIII

Growth Opportunities

Acquisition & Expansion Pipeline

Management has identified two specific acquisition targets for 4th and 5th properties in adjacent Gulf Coast markets. These targets align with the company's waterfront, boutique positioning and geographic expansion strategy. The proven 90-day property integration capability positions the company to rapidly scale from three to five properties within a reasonable timeframe post-transaction.

Corporate & Bleisure Segment Growth

Currently, corporate and bleisure travelers represent only 10% of bookings. Management has identified a clear opportunity to grow this segment to 18–20% of total bookings through targeted sales hiring and marketing. This would involve:

  • Dedicated corporate sales position focused on business travel and corporate retreats.
  • Enhanced group and meeting room offerings to support small to mid-size corporate functions.
  • Partnership development with corporate travel managers and business networks.

Spa Service Expansion

Currently, spa services are offered at the Sarasota and Clearwater properties. Expansion of spa services to the Naples property would increase ancillary revenue and amenity offerings, supporting higher ADR and improved guest satisfaction metrics.

Strategic Partnerships

Management is in discussion with a regional wedding-planning network regarding preferred-venue status. Formalization of this partnership would increase the company's visibility and booking flow within the high-margin wedding and events segment, currently representing 22% of bookings.

Market Penetration

Adjacent Gulf Coast markets present white space for new boutique waterfront properties. The company's proven operating model, brand infrastructure, and management team create a platform for replicable growth in complementary geographic markets.

IX

Risks

Competition & Market Risk

New Branded Competitor Entry (Sarasota, 2027): A new branded hotel is scheduled to enter the Sarasota market in 2027, bringing incremental supply and competitive pressure. Anchor & Vine's differentiation through waterfront positioning, culinary excellence, and boutique service philosophy should mitigate direct cannibalization, though market-wide occupancy compression is possible. This timeline allows for strategic response, such as enhanced marketing or expanded offerings.

Financial & Operational Risks

  • F&B Cost Inflation: F&B operations represent 34% of revenue and are exposed to regional food cost inflation, which could compress margins if not offset by pricing or menu optimization.
  • Weather & Hurricane Exposure: Gulf Coast properties face seasonal hurricane and severe weather risk. This risk is substantially mitigated through $15,000,000 in combined insurance coverage including business interruption protection.

Key Person Risk

The company is founder-led, with Maria Chen and David Okoye driving strategic direction and operational excellence. While management succession planning is underway (Marcus Webb identified as COO successor), further development of a robust executive bench and CEO succession plan will be important post-transaction. Retention of key executives through the transition should be prioritized.

Mitigation Strategies

Management and the business present several inherent strengths that mitigate these risks:

  • 92% occupancy and 41% repeat rate indicate strong market resilience and brand loyalty.
  • Diversified booking channels (38% direct, 35% OTA, 27% repeat/referral) reduce dependency on any single source.
  • Freehold property ownership eliminates lease risk and provides operational control.
  • Proven scalability and rapid integration capability enable geographic diversification and growth.
  • Strong, engaged management team with multi-year tenure and operational excellence track record.
