I

Deal Snapshot

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is a regionally dominant independent boutique hotel operator across Florida's Gulf Coast, comprising three award-winning waterfront properties totaling 212 keys. Founded in 2011, the company has achieved exceptional unit economics—92% occupancy, 41% repeat guest rate, and a robust 27.8% Adjusted EBITDA margin—while generating $14.2 million in FY2025 revenue. The business is anchored by a seasoned founding team, a loyal customer base, and an award-winning food & beverage program that contributes 34% of total revenue.

Investment Highlights

  • Consistent revenue growth: $11.8M (FY2023) → $12.9M (FY2024) → $14.2M (FY2025)
  • Superior occupancy and repeat business: 92% occupancy, 41% repeat guest rate, strong 4.7/5 online reputation across 6,200+ reviews
  • Proven operational scalability: AAA Four Diamond rating at 2 of 3 properties, standardized SOPs enabling 90-day property onboarding
  • F&B excellence: four on-site restaurants and award-winning culinary program drive 34% of revenue; below-market COGS at 28%
  • Prime waterfront real estate: three freehold properties in Clearwater, Sarasota, and Naples; all renovated within past 5 years
  • Founding team commitment: 90-day transition consulting period offered; CEO and COO with 14 years tenure each

Asking Price: $8,500,000

Legal & Compliance

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is organized as a single Delaware LLC holding company with three property-level subsidiaries. This structure provides operational flexibility, liability containment, and tax efficiency. All properties are held in freehold ownership, eliminating long-term lease risk or dependency on third-party landowners.

Licenses & Permits

  • • All liquor, food service, and event licenses are current at all three locations.
  • • No known violations or pending permit issues.
  • • AAA Four Diamond rating maintained at 2 of 3 properties, confirming ongoing compliance with strict quality standards.

Insurance Coverage

Combined general liability and property insurance coverage: $15,000,000

No pending or threatened litigation exists against Anchor & Vine or any of its property entities.

IX

Risk Considerations

Competitive Risks

  • • New Branded Competitor (Sarasota, 2027): A new branded hotel is confirmed to enter the Sarasota market in 2027 with greater capital resources and national brand recognition. This may compress pricing in a key market segment and increase marketing/acquisition costs.
  • • Regional Competition: Ongoing competition from larger independent operators and select-service branded chains limits pricing power in certain periods.

Operational & Financial Risks

  • • Weather & Hurricane Exposure: Gulf Coast properties are exposed to seasonal hurricanes and weather events that may disrupt operations, damage property, and elevate F&B procurement costs. Insurance coverage mitigates but does not eliminate this risk.
  • • Founder Dependency: CEO Maria Chen and COO David Okoye have each served 14 years with the company and are instrumental to its strategic vision, operational culture, and market relationships. Transition risk exists despite identified internal succession and post-closing consulting support.
  • • Seasonal Revenue Volatility: Leisure-driven business model (68% of bookings) creates seasonal demand fluctuations, with higher occupancy in winter months and potential softness in summer.

Strategic Risks

  • • Limited Group Space: Identified weakness in group and conference capabilities relative to larger competitors may limit market share in corporate and group event segments, despite strong positioning in small weddings.
  • • Integration Risk: While standardized processes support rapid property onboarding, execution of acquisition strategy depends on successful identification, negotiation, and integration of additional Gulf Coast assets.
