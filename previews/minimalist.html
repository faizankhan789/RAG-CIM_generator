II.

The Business

History & Ownership

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group was founded in 2011 by hospitality operators Maria Chen and David Okoye. Over 14 years, the company grew from a single property to a portfolio of three freehold waterfront hotels across Florida's Gulf Coast. The company remains 100% privately held with no outside investors, allowing management to prioritize long-term value creation and guest-centric operations over short-term financial pressures.

Properties & Locations

Portfolio Overview

  • • Clearwater, FL — Waterfront boutique property with spa services
  • • Sarasota, FL — Premium beachfront location with event venues
  • • Naples, FL — Luxury waterfront destination with spa services

Key Facilities

  • • 212 total guest keys across 3 properties
  • • 4 on-site restaurants & F&B outlets
  • • Wedding & event venues at all 3 properties
  • • Spa services at 2 properties (Clearwater, Naples)

All three properties are freehold owned and have been fully renovated within the last five years, positioning the portfolio as modern, well-maintained, and guest-ready. Prime waterfront locations provide significant competitive moat against new entrants and enhance pricing power.

Competitive Advantages

  • Prime Waterfront Locations: Freehold ownership of three of the most desirable waterfront sites on Florida's Gulf Coast creates defensible competitive moat
  • Guest Reputation & Loyalty: 4.7/5 average rating across 6,200+ reviews and 41% repeat guest rate demonstrate strong brand equity and customer satisfaction
  • Experienced Management: Long-tenured General Managers at each property provide stability, local market knowledge, and continuity
  • Award-Winning F&B Program: Multi-outlet restaurant portfolio contributes 34% of total revenue and drives guest experience differentiation
  • Unified Brand Recognition: 2019 rebrand created cohesive "Anchor & Vine" identity across all three properties, enhancing marketing efficiency

Values & Market Position

Anchor & Vine operates on three core values: guest-first service, sustainable sourcing, and community partnership. This philosophy translates into operational practices including local F&B sourcing, consistent service standards, and active community engagement. The company holds #2 market position among independent boutique operators in the region by RevPAR, competing on brand strength and experience rather than scale alone.

IX.

Risk Factors

Industry & Competitive Risks

New Branded Competitor (Sarasota, 2027)

A new branded hotel operator has announced plans to enter the Sarasota market in 2027. This competitor may have brand recognition, loyalty program integration, and scale advantages. Mitigation strategies include continued emphasis on boutique differentiation, guest loyalty programs, and superior service standards that outweigh price competition.

Financial & Operational Risks

Food Cost Inflation

Regional food cost inflation poses margin pressure on 34% of revenue from F&B operations. Mitigation includes long-term vendor contracts (updated annually), menu pricing flexibility, and procurement optimization.

Weather & Hurricane Exposure

Gulf Coast location exposes properties to hurricane and severe weather risk. Fully insured with $15M combined liability and property coverage. Detailed disaster recovery and business continuity plans are in place.

Management & Succession Risks

Key Person Dependency: Anchor & Vine is founder-led by Maria Chen (CEO) and David Okoye (COO), each with 14 years in the business. While Marcus Webb has been identified as internal successor to the COO role, formal succession planning for CEO position is in early stages. New ownership should prioritize management retention and formalized succession protocols to ensure continuity.

Strategic Considerations

  • Limited Group / Conference Space: Portfolio lacks large group meeting facilities, limiting corporate conference market opportunity. Mitigation includes targeted small-meeting market and strategic partnerships.
  • Scale vs. Competitors: Smaller size vs. branded competitors requires operational excellence and differentiation to compete effectively on non-price factors.
