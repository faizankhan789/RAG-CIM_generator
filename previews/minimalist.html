Snapshot

Business overview, investment thesis, and key financial metrics

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is a premium boutique operator managing a carefully curated collection of three waterfront hotels across Florida's Gulf Coast. Since its founding in 2011, the company has built a reputation for exceptional guest experiences, award-winning food and beverage offerings, and a commitment to sustainable hospitality. The business is positioned as the #2 independent boutique operator by RevPAR in its region, with a track record of strong occupancy, high repeat guest loyalty, and meaningful EBITDA margins.

Investment Highlights

  • Proven Operations & Scale: 14-year operating history with consistent revenue growth (18% CAGR FY2023–FY2025). 212 total keys across 3 freehold, waterfront properties.
  • Operational Excellence: 92% average occupancy, 41% repeat guest rate, RevPAR of $186, and strong online reputation (4.7/5 across 6,200+ reviews).
  • Diversified Revenue Streams: Rooms (62.7%), F&B (34.0%), and Spa & Other (3.3%). Award-winning culinary program drives premiumization and guest differentiation.
  • Strong Financial Profile: FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA of $3.95M at a 27.8% margin. Conservative debt load ($2.1M at 5.4% fixed, maturing 2031).
  • Scalable Platform: Standardized SOPs and training program enabling new-property integration within 90 days. Clear expansion roadmap with 2 identified acquisition targets.
  • Experienced Ownership & Management: Founder-led team with 14-year tenure, a 24% employee turnover rate (vs. 35% hospitality average), and a named internal successor for COO.

The Business

Company history, ownership, values, management team, facilities, and competitive positioning

Company Foundation & Growth

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group was founded in 2011 by hospitality entrepreneurs Maria Chen and David Okoye. What began as a single waterfront property has grown into a carefully managed collection of three boutique hotels across Florida's Gulf Coast. Over the past 14 years, the founders have built a business defined by a guest-first philosophy, commitment to sustainable sourcing, and deep community partnerships.

The company remains 100% privately held with no external investors. This ownership structure has enabled the founders to maintain long-term strategic vision, prioritize guest and employee satisfaction over short-term margin maximization, and invest in brand development and operational excellence.

Business at a Glance

  • Founded: 2011
  • Founders: Maria Chen & David Okoye
  • Growth Path: 1 → 3 properties in 14 years
  • Ownership: 100% Private
  • Regions: Gulf Coast, Florida

Facilities & Property Portfolio

Anchor & Vine operates three freehold waterfront properties totaling 212 guestroom keys, all renovated within the past 5 years. Each property occupies a prime waterfront location and features distinct personality aligned with its local market while maintaining unified brand standards.

Clearwater Property

Waterfront location with spa services, on-site restaurant, event venue, and conference facilities. Recently renovated with modern amenities and high-demand beach access.

Sarasota Property

Cultural hub with upscale restaurant, wedding venue, and event space. Strong positioning in the artsy, affluent Sarasota market with robust repeat guest base.

Naples Property

Luxury positioning in Naples' affluent market with spa services, upscale F&B, and premium event facilities. Strong corporate and high-net-worth guest appeal.

Products & Services

  • Guestrooms & Suites: 212 keys across three properties, ranging from standard to premium waterfront suites with high occupancy.
  • Food & Beverage: Four on-site restaurants across the portfolio, featuring award-winning culinary programs, sustainable sourcing, and diverse cuisine concepts.
  • Event & Wedding Venues: Waterfront event spaces at all three properties, catering to weddings, corporate retreats, and private celebrations.
  • Spa & Wellness: Full-service spa facilities at two properties (Clearwater and Naples), with opportunity to expand to the third property (Sarasota).
  • Guest Experiences: Curated activities, local partnerships, and personalized concierge services driving premium positioning and repeat bookings.

Competitive Advantages

Anchor & Vine operates with several distinct competitive moats:

  • Prime Waterfront Locations: Freehold ownership of three premium Gulf Coast properties with limited new supply due to land scarcity and regulatory constraints.
  • Renowned Guest Experiences: 4.7/5 average online rating across 6,200+ reviews. 41% repeat guest rate and strong referral engine demonstrate brand loyalty and word-of-mouth power.
  • Long-Tenured General Managers: Experienced, stable property leadership at each location, reducing turnover and maintaining operational continuity.
  • Award-Winning F&B Program: Culinary differentiation that commands premium pricing and drives incremental revenue through dining, events, and corporate partnerships.
  • Scalable Operations Platform: Standardized SOPs, centralized revenue management (Cloudbeds PMS), and robust training programs enable efficient new-property integration and consistent brand delivery.

Market Position & Industry Standing

Anchor & Vine holds the position of #2 independent boutique operator in its region by RevPAR. The company competes alongside other independent properties and small regional chains, differentiated by its premium branding, consistent guest satisfaction, and operational excellence. The Gulf Coast hotel market remains resilient, with strong leisure demand, stable corporate travel, and growing destination wedding segment.

Two of three properties hold AAA Four Diamond Ratings, a hallmark of premium hospitality quality and guest service standards.

How It Runs

Operations, technology infrastructure, supply chain, quality standards, and scalability

Technology & Systems Infrastructure

Anchor & Vine operates a modern, integrated technology stack designed to optimize revenue management, streamline operations, and enhance guest experience across all three properties.

Property Management System

Cloudbeds PMS — Cloud-based platform providing centralized reservation management, guest profiles, rate management, and reporting across all three properties. Enables real-time occupancy and revenue visibility.

Revenue Management

Centralized revenue management system optimizing pricing, inventory allocation, and channel distribution. Dynamic rate adjustments based on demand, competition, and market conditions.

Quality Standards & Certifications

  • AAA Four Diamond Rating: Achieved at 2 of 3 properties, certifying superior guest experience, property maintenance, and service standards.
  • All Licenses Current: Liquor, food service, and event licenses maintained at all locations, ensuring compliance with state and local regulations.
  • Continuous Improvement Programs: Regular guest satisfaction surveys, staff training initiatives, and property audits to maintain quality benchmarks.

Scalability & Growth Framework

Anchor & Vine has developed a scalable operational platform enabling efficient integration of new properties. The company maintains standardized Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) covering guest services, F&B operations, housekeeping, maintenance, and revenue management. A comprehensive training program enables new property teams to achieve operational proficiency within 90 days of acquisition.

This capability positions the company to execute its growth agenda rapidly, whether acquiring additional properties or expanding services (e.g., spa expansion to Sarasota).

Scaling Capabilities

  • Standardized SOPs across all locations
  • Centralized vendor relationships
  • Unified PMS and revenue systems
  • Experienced management team
  • 90-day new property onboarding
  • Cross-property resource sharing

Supply Chain & Vendor Relationships

Food & beverage sourcing is managed through partnerships with six regional vendors under annual contracts. This diversified vendor base reduces supply chain risk and maintains competitive pricing while supporting local economies—aligned with the company's sustainability values.

  • Vendor Concentration: No single vendor exceeds 18% of COGS, mitigating supply disruption risk.
  • Local Sourcing: Emphasis on regional and local suppliers where quality and pricing are competitive, supporting community relationships and brand values.
  • Annual Contracting: Multi-year agreements with key suppliers ensure pricing stability and supply continuity.

The company operates in a region exposed to hurricane and severe weather events. All three properties carry comprehensive insurance coverage:

Insurance Coverage

$15,000,000 combined general liability and property coverage protects against guest liability, property damage, business interruption, and loss of revenue in the event of covered disasters.

Compliance

Legal structure, permits & licenses, insurance, and regulatory standing

Legal Structure

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group operates as a single Delaware LLC holding company with three property-level subsidiaries, one for each location (Clearwater, Sarasota, Naples). This structure provides liability separation and operational flexibility while maintaining centralized ownership and control.

Permits & Licenses

All required permits and licenses are current at all three locations:

  • Liquor licenses (on-premise consumption at all restaurant/bar facilities)
  • Food service licenses (kitchen operations and F&B service)
  • Event/catering licenses (wedding and private event hosting)
  • Hotel/lodging licenses (guest accommodation operations)
  • Local zoning and business operating permits

Insurance Coverage

General Liability

Covers bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury claims arising from business operations, guest incidents, and employee actions.

Property Insurance

Covers physical assets (buildings, furnishings, equipment) against fire, theft, weather damage, and other covered perils. Includes business interruption coverage.

Total Insurance Coverage

$15,000,000 combined general liability and property coverage provides comprehensive protection for the three-property portfolio. Hurricane and windstorm coverage is included, appropriate for Gulf Coast operations.

Litigation & Legal Standing

No pending or threatened litigation exists against the company, its properties, or management team. The business maintains clean legal standing with all regulatory authorities.

Risk Factors

Competitive, operational, and strategic risks facing the business

Competitive Risks

New Market Competition

A new branded hotel is entering the Sarasota market in 2027, bringing increased competition for leisure, event, and corporate bookings. This could pressure occupancy and rates.

OTA Dependency

35% of bookings come from OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia). Changes in OTA commission rates, algorithms, or competitive positioning could impact booking volume and margins.

Operational Risks

Weather & Natural Disasters

Gulf Coast properties are exposed to hurricane, flooding, and severe weather events. While fully insured, operational disruptions and guest cancellations could impact revenue and guest satisfaction.

Labor Market Tightness

Hospitality labor market remains competitive. Wage pressures and difficulty recruiting skilled workers (chefs, housekeeping, management) could increase labor costs and impact service quality.

Financial Risks

F&B Cost Inflation

Food and beverage costs are exposed to regional price inflation and commodity volatility. While vendor diversification mitigates risk, significant inflation could compress F&B margins.

Macroeconomic Sensitivity

Leisure travel and destination events are sensitive to economic downturns, consumer confidence, and discretionary spending. A recession could reduce bookings and occupancy.

Management & Succession Risks

Founder Dependency

CEO and COO roles are founder-led (Maria Chen and David Okoye). While a succession plan exists for COO, the CEO role remains dependent on the founder. Transition to new ownership and management could present execution risks.

Key Person Risk

Loss of key General Managers or property-level leaders could disrupt operations, guest relationships, and staff morale. Mitigation includes strong bench strength, cross-training, and documented processes.

Mitigation Strategies

  • Insurance: Comprehensive $15M coverage protects against property and liability risks, including weather and business interruption.
  • Operational Scale: Three-property portfolio reduces dependency on single location. Diversified revenue streams (rooms, F&B, events, spa) reduce booking concentration risk.
  • Guest Loyalty: 41% repeat guest rate and 4.7/5 online rating create resilience in competitive environment. Strong brand loyalty supports pricing power.
  • Vendor Diversification: Six F&B suppliers with no vendor >18% of COGS reduces supply chain risk.
  • Direct Bookings: 38% of bookings from direct website reduce OTA dependency and improve margin capture.
