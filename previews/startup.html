Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is a boutique collection of three award-winning waterfront hotels (212 keys) across Florida's Gulf Coast, generating $14.2 million in FY2025 revenue with exceptional 27.8% Adjusted EBITDA margins. II

What We Do

Business Overview

Founded in 2011 by hospitality operators Maria Chen and David Okoye, Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group has grown from a single property to a curated portfolio of three premium waterfront hotels operating under a unified brand since 2019. The company combines guest-first service, sustainable sourcing, and community partnership to deliver distinctive experiences across the Gulf Coast.

Products & Services

  • ●Upscale guest rooms (212 keys across 3 properties)
  • ●Four award-winning on-site restaurants
  • ●Event and wedding venue services
  • ●Spa services at 2 of 3 properties

Geographic Footprint

  • ●Clearwater, FL – Waterfront property
  • ●Sarasota, FL – Waterfront property
  • ●Naples, FL – Waterfront property

Competitive Advantages

  • Prime waterfront locations in premium leisure markets
  • Long-tenured, experienced GMs at each property
  • Brand reputation: 4.7/5 average rating (6,200+ reviews)
  • #2 independent boutique operator by RevPAR in region
  • AAA Four Diamond certification (2 of 3 properties)
  • Unified brand identity driving recognition and loyalty

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Strong brand reputation and guest loyalty
  • High occupancy rates (92%) and premium ADR ($202)
  • Freehold property ownership (no lease constraints)
  • Diversified revenue streams (rooms, F&B, events)
  • Experienced management team with long tenure

Weaknesses

  • Limited group/conference space—constrains corporate/large event segments
  • Founder-led management structure—key person risk in CEO/COO roles
  • Smaller portfolio vs. regional competitors
  • Spa services available at only 2 of 3 properties

Opportunities

  • 4th and 5th property acquisition pipeline in adjacent Gulf Coast markets
  • Spa expansion to Clearwater property
  • Strategic partnership with regional wedding-planning networks
  • Corporate/bleisure segment growth from 10% to 18-20% of bookings
  • Scale operational excellence and brand

Threats

  • New branded hotel competitor entering Sarasota market in 2027
  • Regional food cost inflation exposure (F&B is 34% of revenue)
  • Hurricane and coastal weather risk (fully insured)
  • Competitive pressure from larger boutique chains

Organization & Culture

Headcount: 168 employees (94 full-time, 74 part-time) across 3 properties. The organization prioritizes guest-first service, sustainable sourcing, and community partnership. Benefits include health insurance for full-time staff, 401(k) with 3% match, and F&B staff meal program. Employee turnover rate of 24% annually is well below the hospitality industry average (~35%), reflecting strong culture and retention.

Legal Structure: Single Delaware LLC holding company with 3 property-level subsidiaries. 100% privately held with no outside investors, ensuring operational control and alignment.

IV

How It Works

Technology & Systems

Property Management System: Cloud-based Cloudbeds PMS provides unified guest management, reservations, and operational insights across all 3 properties. Revenue Management: Centralized revenue management system optimizes pricing and occupancy in real time.

These integrated systems enable data-driven decision-making, enhance guest experience, and facilitate rapid onboarding of new properties.

Operations Highlights

  • Standardized SOPs across all 3 properties
  • Structured training program for new staff
  • 90-day new-property onboarding timeline
  • Cloud-based system architecture
  • Real-time data analytics & reporting

Supply Chain & Sourcing

F&B sourcing is managed through relationships with six regional vendors, all under annual contracts. This diversified vendor base ensures competitive pricing, supply resilience, and sustainability. No single vendor exceeds 18% of COGS, mitigating supply-chain risk and maintaining vendor leverage.

Quality & Certifications

  • AAA Four Diamond Rating: Two of three properties hold prestigious AAA Four Diamond certification, signaling premium guest experience and service standards
  • All Permits & Licenses Current: Liquor, food service, and event licenses are current and in good standing at all locations
  • Recent Renovations: All three properties have been renovated within the last 5 years, ensuring modern amenities and competitive market positioning

Scalability & Growth Infrastructure

The company has built a highly scalable operational model:

  • Standardized Playbook: Documented SOPs and training programs enable consistent quality and rapid deployment across new properties
  • 90-Day Onboarding: New properties can be integrated and fully operational within 90 days, supported by proven systems and experienced management
  • Cloud-First Architecture: Technology infrastructure is designed for scalability, allowing easy addition of new locations without major capital investment in IT
  • Vendor Relationships: Established supply-chain partnerships extend to new markets, supporting cost efficiency and quality consistency

IX

Risks & Mitigations

Key Risk Factors

Competitive Pressure
Market Risk
Risk: New branded hotel competitor entering Sarasota market in 2027 could pressure pricing and occupancy.

Mitigation: Strong brand reputation (4.7/5 rating), 41% repeat-guest rate, and waterfront location differentiate Anchor & Vine. Premium positioning and guest loyalty provide resilience.
F&B Cost Inflation
Financial Risk
Risk: Regional food cost inflation could compress F&B margins; F&B represents 34% of revenue.

Mitigation: Diversified vendor base (6 vendors, no single vendor >18% of COGS) provides pricing leverage. Ability to adjust menu pricing given premium positioning.
Weather & Coastal Risk
Operational Risk
Risk: Hurricane/weather exposure typical of Gulf Coast coastal properties could disrupt operations and damage assets.

Mitigation: Comprehensive insurance coverage ($15M combined liability + property). All properties renovated within last 5 years with modern construction standards.
Key Person Risk
Operational Risk
Risk: Founder-led management (Maria Chen as CEO, David Okoye as COO) creates succession risk in top roles.

Mitigation: VP Operations identified as internal COO successor (4-year tenure). Management team structure provides continuity depth.
Debt Service
Financial Risk
Risk: $2.1M mortgage outstanding at 5.4% fixed rate through 2031; interest rate environment may tighten refinancing costs.

Mitigation: Fixed-rate debt reduces refinancing risk. Strong EBITDA ($3.95M FY2025) provides robust debt-service coverage (1.9x).
Segment Concentration
Market Risk
Risk: Leisure segment represents 68% of bookings; economic downturn could reduce discretionary travel demand.

Mitigation: Diversification into corporate/bleisure (10% → 20% opportunity) and events (22%) reduces leisure dependence. Strong repeat-guest rate (41%) provides booking stability.

Risk Assessment & Control Environment

Insurance & Compliance: Comprehensive insurance portfolio, current permits/licenses, no litigation, and clean regulatory record demonstrate strong risk management practices.

Financial Controls: Experienced CFO (Priya Anand, 6-year tenure), cloud-based financial systems, and standardized reporting provide visibility and control over financial risks.
