Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group is a high-performing, owner-operated collection of three premier waterfront boutique hotels generating consistent cash flow and premium returns on the Gulf Coast. With 212 keys across Clearwater, Sarasota, and Naples, Florida, the company achieves 92% occupancy, a 41% repeat guest rate, and a best-in-class 27.8% Adjusted EBITDA margin. This is a differentiated acquisition opportunity for a strategic or financial buyer seeking a stabilized, profitable hospitality platform with minimal leverage and significant upside through operational leverage and market expansion.

What We Do

History & Founding

Anchor & Vine Hospitality Group was founded in 2011 by Maria Chen and David Okoye, veteran hospitality operators with a shared vision of creating distinctive, guest-centric waterfront properties. The company began with a single property and has systematically grown to a portfolio of three premium boutique hotels over the past fourteen years. This disciplined, founder-led approach has ensured consistent quality, brand integrity, and operational excellence across all properties.

Ownership & Structure

The company is 100% privately held with no external investors. It is organized as a single Delaware LLC holding company with three property-level subsidiaries, providing operational autonomy and tax efficiency. This structure is founder-controlled and founder-managed, with a clear strategic vision and decision-making authority concentrated in an experienced, aligned leadership team.

Core Values

  • Guest-first service: Every decision is guided by delivering exceptional, personalized hospitality experiences.
  • Sustainable sourcing: Local, ethically sourced ingredients and eco-conscious operations across all properties.
  • Community partnership: Active involvement in local Gulf Coast business and charitable initiatives.

Products & Services

  • Rooms: 212 total keys (mix of suites, standard rooms, and premium accommodations) across three properties, all renovated within the last 5 years.

  • Events & Weddings: Full-service event planning and hosting; small to mid-sized wedding and corporate event venues at all three properties.
  • Spa Services: Premium spa offerings at Sarasota and Naples properties; expansion planned for Clearwater.

Risks & Mitigations

Key Risk Factors

Competition & Market Entry

Risk: A new branded hotel competitor is planned to enter the Sarasota market in 2027, potentially capturing market share and driving down rates in that market.

Mitigation: Anchor & Vine's strong brand reputation, repeat guest loyalty (41%), and premium positioning create customer stickiness. The company's diversified portfolio (three properties across different markets) reduces dependence on any single market. Enhanced corporate/bleisure focus and wedding event positioning further differentiate from traditional branded chains.

Input Cost Inflation (F&B)

Risk: Regional food and beverage costs are exposed to inflation pressures, which could squeeze F&B margins (currently 28% COGS).

Mitigation: Diversified vendor base (six regional suppliers with no single vendor exceeding 18% of COGS) provides negotiating leverage and supply flexibility. Strong menu engineering and pricing power (premium positioning) allow the company to pass through modest cost increases to guests. Local sourcing strategy provides partial hedge against commodity inflation.

Geographic & Weather Exposure

Risk: All three properties are located on Florida's Gulf Coast and are exposed to hurricane and severe weather risk.

Mitigation: All properties are fully insured with $15,000,000 combined general liability and property coverage, including hurricane/windstorm protection. Properties have been renovated within the last 5 years to modern building codes. While risk is inherent to coastal Gulf Coast operations, insurance and property quality mitigate financial impact.

Key Person Risk

Risk: CEO (Maria Chen) and COO (David Okoye) have been with the company for its entire 14-year history and are closely associated with brand and strategy. Loss of either founder could disrupt operations and guest relationships.

Mitigation: VP Operations Marcus Webb has been identified as the internal successor to the COO role and has been with the company for 4 years. A 90-day transition consulting period is contractually available from the sellers post-close. Standardized SOPs and trained management teams at each property reduce founder dependency. Documented systems and processes enable continuity.

Labor Market Tightness

Risk: Hospitality sector faces ongoing labor supply challenges, potentially pushing wages higher and compressing margins.

Mitigation: Anchor & Vine's 24% annual employee turnover is significantly below the industry average (35%), indicating strong workforce retention and satisfaction. Competitive benefits, professional development opportunities, and positive workplace culture are competitive advantages in labor recruitment and retention.

Economic Downturn / Demand Decline

Risk: A macro recession or significant reduction in leisure travel demand could reduce bookings, occupancy, and room rates.

Mitigation: The 41% repeat guest rate and strong brand loyalty indicate resilience during downturns. Diversified customer base (68% leisure, 22% events, 10% corporate) and geographic spread reduce concentration risk. Premium positioning and freehold ownership (no rent burden) provide flexibility to adjust operations if needed.
